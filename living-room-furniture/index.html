Living room furniture: what to know

The living room is where size mistakes show most. A sofa that is a few centimetres too long blocks a door, and a TV unit that is too deep takes floor space you wanted for walking. Every piece below is made to order, so you give us the wall and we plan the furniture around it.

Planning a living room

Start with the sofa set, because the other pieces follow its size. Measure the wall it will stand against and the width of the door it has to pass through. Keep a walking path of about 80 cm between the sofas and the other furniture, and about 40 cm between the sofa and the centre table.

For the TV unit, tell us the screen size and where the sockets are. A 55 inch screen is about 122 cm wide, so the unit should be a little wider than that, with space on both sides.

What you can change

  • Sofa fabric, colour, arm and back style, and how firm the seat feels.
  • TV unit width, number of shelves and cable gaps at the back.
  • Centre table shape, height and whether it has a lower shelf.
  • Wood tone and finish, so all three pieces match.

Questions about living room furniture

Can I order a sofa set in a different size?

Yes. The standard set is one three seater and two singles. Send us your wall length and door width and we adjust the size.

Can the sofa, TV unit and centre table match?

Yes. We can make all three in the same wood tone and finish, so the room looks planned.
