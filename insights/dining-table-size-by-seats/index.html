Dining table size: how big for 4, 6 or 8 people

Allow about 60 cm of table edge for each person and about 90 cm behind each chair. Then test the size against your room.

Written by the team at Muhammad Furnitures. Published 28 November 2026.

Short answer: Allow about 60 cm of table edge for each person and about 90 cm behind each chair. Then test the size against your room.

Start with the people

Each person needs roughly 60 cm of table edge to eat without bumping elbows. Here are rough sizes for a rectangular table:

  • 4 people: about 120 × 75 cm.
  • 6 people: about 150 to 180 × 90 cm. Our 6 seater dining table is 180 × 90 × 76 cm.
  • 8 people: about 200 to 240 × 90 to 100 cm.

These are guides, not rules. Chairs with arms need more room, and so do bigger families.

Test it in your room

  1. Mark the table size on the floor with tape or sheets of newspaper.
  2. Leave about 90 cm behind each chair so it can slide back.
  3. If people walk behind the chairs, leave about 120 cm.
  4. Stand where the door opens and check you can still get in and out.

How to measure a room covers the rest of the numbers to write down.

Choose the shape

  • Rectangular tables seat the most people for the length.
  • A round table of about 100 to 120 cm suits four people in a square room, and no one sits in a corner.
  • In a long, narrow room, a narrower rectangular table leaves a better walking path.

Height and legs

Most dining tables stand about 74 to 76 cm high, and chairs with a seat around 45 cm high go with them. Look at where the legs are. Legs set at the corners leave more room for knees, and a leg set in from the end can block someone seated there.

If you host a crowd only sometimes

Tell us how often you have a full table. A slightly longer table, or a separate side table for big days, can work better than a large table that fills the room every day.

Common questions

How many people sit at a 180 × 90 cm table?

Six, with three on each long side. Two more can sit at the ends if the legs allow it.

What size should dining chairs be?

A seat about 45 cm from the floor suits a table about 75 cm high. That leaves roughly 28 to 30 cm between the seat and the tabletop.

Can the table be made to my size?

Yes. Send the room size and how many people you seat, and we reply with a price.
