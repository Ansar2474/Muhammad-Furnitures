Sheesham, kikar and deodar: which wood for which piece

Sheesham is dense and polishes well. Kikar is hard and heavy. Deodar is lighter and softer. The right one depends on the piece, the room and your budget.

Written by the team at Muhammad Furnitures. Published 25 November 2026.

Short answer: Sheesham is dense and polishes well. Kikar is hard and heavy. Deodar is lighter and softer. The right one depends on the piece, the room and your budget.

Sheesham

Sheesham is a dense hardwood with a brown colour that ranges from golden to dark, and a clear grain. It is strong, takes a polish well, and can be sanded and polished again years later. It usually costs more than the other two. It suits pieces that take daily use, such as dining tables, bed frames and desks.

Kikar

Kikar is also a hard, heavy wood. It is often used for frames, legs and solid pieces. Its grain is more uneven than sheesham, and it can have knots. Whether a kikar piece stays flat depends a great deal on how well the wood was dried before it was cut, so ask your maker about that.

Deodar

Deodar is a softwood with a light colour and a pleasant smell. It is lighter than sheesham or kikar and easy to work. Because it is softer, it dents more easily. It is a common choice for doors, windows and simple furniture where weight and cost matter.

Wood or board?

Many good pieces use both. A dining table top in solid wood, for example, and the inside of a wardrobe in plywood. The next article explains where each one makes sense.

Questions to ask before you pay

  • Which wood or board is used for each part of the piece?
  • How was the wood dried before it was cut?
  • What finish goes on it: natural polish, stain or paint?
  • Can I see the wood or a sample of the finish before you start?

A maker who has clear answers to these is worth talking to. We are happy to answer them for any piece we build.

Common questions

Which wood is best for furniture?

There is no single best wood. Sheesham is a strong and popular choice for pieces that get daily use. The right wood depends on the piece, the room and your budget.

Can I order a piece in a wood I choose?

Yes. Tell us which wood you want or what your budget is, and we suggest options and price them.
