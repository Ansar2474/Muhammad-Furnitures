How to plan the inside of a wardrobe

List what you store, measure your longest garment, and give each group its own space: hanging, folded, drawers and a shelf for bags and bedding.

Written by the team at Muhammad Furnitures. Published 28 November 2026.

Short answer: List what you store, measure your longest garment, and give each group its own space: hanging, folded, drawers and a shelf for bags and bedding.

Start with what you own

Before you choose a design, make a rough list: how many long garments, how many shirts, how much folded clothing, bedding, bags and shoes. The inside of the wardrobe should follow that list, not a standard layout.

Hanging space

Measure your longest garment on its hanger, then add about 10 cm so it does not touch the floor of the wardrobe. Long kameez, coats and wedding clothes need full height. Shirts and short kameez need about half, so one tall rail and one lower pair of rails can hold more than a single rail.

Shelves and drawers

  • Shelves about 35 to 40 cm apart suit folded clothes and keep stacks from getting too high.
  • Drawers suit small items such as socks, scarves and jewellery boxes.
  • A top shelf holds bedding, suitcases and things you use rarely.
  • A narrow section with shelves suits bags and belts.

Doors

Hinged doors need space in front of the wardrobe to open. If the room is tight, say so and we talk through the layout. A mirror on a door saves wall space in a small bedroom.

What to send us

Send the wall width and height, a photo, and your list of what you store. We plan the inside with you and send a price. The standard 3 door wardrobe is 150 × 60 × 210 cm and can be changed to fit.

Common questions

How deep should a wardrobe be?

About 55 to 60 cm suits clothes on hangers. Our standard 3 door wardrobe is 60 cm deep. Tell us if your space is shallower.

Can I have drawers inside the wardrobe?

Yes. Tell us how many you want and what they will hold.
