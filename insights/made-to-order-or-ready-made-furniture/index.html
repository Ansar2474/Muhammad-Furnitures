Made-to-order or ready-made furniture: which one should you buy?

Ready-made is faster and lets you see the piece first. Made-to-order fits your room and your choices better. Pick by how much your room needs a particular size.

Written by the team at Muhammad Furnitures. Published 24 November 2026.

Short answer: Ready-made is faster and lets you see the piece first. Made-to-order fits your room and your choices better. Pick by how much your room needs a particular size.

What ready-made gives you

  • You see the real piece before you buy.
  • You can usually take it home sooner.
  • The price is fixed and easy to compare.

Ready-made is a good choice when your room suits standard sizes and you are not choosing a particular colour or finish.

What made-to-order gives you

  • A size that fits your wall, your mattress or your door.
  • Your choice of wood or board, fabric, colour and finish.
  • Details you can ask for, such as drawers in a desk or the layout inside a wardrobe.

It suits rooms with an unusual shape, low ceilings, alcoves or a mattress that is not a standard size.

What to check with made-to-order

  1. Ask for the price in writing, with the size, materials and finish named.
  2. Ask how long the piece will take, and when you will be told if that changes.
  3. Ask how payment works and when it is due.
  4. Ask what happens if the piece does not match what was agreed.

Our standard is a price before any work starts, with the details in writing. See how custom orders work.

How to decide

If a standard size fits and you like what you see, ready-made is simple. If you have measured and the standard sizes leave gaps, or you want a finish you cannot find, made-to-order is worth the wait. Send us your room size and we tell you honestly whether you need a custom size at all.

Common questions

Is made-to-order furniture more expensive?

Not always. The price depends on size, materials and finish. We price the piece you describe, so you can compare it with a ready-made one.

Can I change an order after I place it?

Tell us as early as you can. Changes made before work starts are easy, and the price changes with them.
