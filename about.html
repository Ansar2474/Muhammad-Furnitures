About Muhammad Furnitures

Muhammad Furnitures is a local furniture maker. We build beds, sofas, wardrobes and tables for homes, shops and offices.

We work with you from the first measurement to the final polish. You tell us what you need, we suggest the right design and materials, and our craftsmen build it. Because we make everything ourselves, we can adjust sizes, colours and finishes to match your space and your budget.

We care about simple things: strong joints, clean finishing, honest prices and delivery on the day we promise.
