About Muhammad Furnitures

Muhammad Furnitures makes furniture to order. Beds, sofas, wardrobes, tables and desks, for homes, shops and offices.

You tell us the space and the budget. We suggest a design and the materials, then build it. Because we make each piece ourselves, changing a size or a colour is easy to ask for.

What we aim for is simple: a piece that fits, a price you know before we start, and a finish you picked.

What we build

Sofa sets, beds with storage, wardrobes, dining tables, office desks, TV units, centre tables, dressing tables and bookshelves. Each one is made to order, so the size, the colour and the finish are yours to decide. You can see the starting prices in the shop and the rooms in the collections.

How we work with you

Every order starts with a conversation on WhatsApp or by phone. You send the size of the space and a photo, and we suggest a design and name the materials. You get the price in writing. If it suits you, we start. When the piece is ready, we arrange delivery.

If you are unsure about a size, a wood or a finish, ask. We would rather answer a question before the work than fix a misunderstanding after it.

What you can expect from us

  • A price before any work starts.
  • Straight answers about wood, board and finish when you ask.
  • A piece made to the size you gave us.
  • A message from us if anything about your order changes.
