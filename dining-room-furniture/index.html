Dining room furniture: what to know

Size the table for the people who eat there every day, not for the biggest dinner of the year. Plan about 60 cm of table edge for each person. Six people, three on each long side, need a table about 180 cm long.

Planning a dining room

Leave about 90 cm of free floor between the table and the wall, so chairs can slide back and people can walk behind them. Measure from the table edge to the wall before you choose a length.

If the room is small, a narrower table or one with a shorter length often works better than the standard size. We make both.

What you can change

  • Length and width of the top.
  • Wood tone, colour and finish.
  • Number and style of chairs, made to order with the table.
  • Leg style, if you have one in mind.

Questions about dining room furniture

What size dining table seats six people?

A table about 180 × 90 cm seats six, with three people on each long side. That is the standard size of our 6 seater dining table.

Can you make the chairs too?

Yes. Chairs can be made to order with the table, in the same finish.

Can the table be longer or shorter?

Yes. Tell us the room size and how many people eat there, and we suggest a length.
