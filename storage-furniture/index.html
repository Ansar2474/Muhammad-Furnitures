Storage furniture: what to know

Storage furniture has to fit the wall it stands against and hold what you own. Floors and ceilings are rarely level, so measure the wall height at both ends. Tell us what you store, and we plan the hanging space, shelves and drawers to match.

Planning storage

A hinged wardrobe door needs space in front of it to open. If the room is tight, tell us and we talk through other layouts.

For a bookshelf, say what it will hold. Paperbacks need less depth than files or large books, and heavy books need shelves that do not sag. Fix tall shelves to the wall so they cannot tip.

What you can change

  • Width and height, to fit your wall.
  • Hanging space, shelves and drawers inside a wardrobe.
  • A mirror on a wardrobe door.
  • Shelf spacing and shelf depth on a bookshelf.
  • Colour and finish.

Questions about storage furniture

Can the inside of the wardrobe be planned?

Yes. Tell us what you store and we plan the hanging space, shelves and drawers with you.

Can the wardrobe be made for an uneven wall or ceiling?

Yes. Send the measurements at both ends of the wall and a photo, and we plan around it.

Can I order a bookshelf in a different size?

Yes. Give us the width and height of the space and what the shelves will hold.
