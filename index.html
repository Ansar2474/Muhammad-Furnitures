Muhammad Furnitures makes sofa sets, beds, wardrobes, dining tables, office desks, TV units and centre tables for homes, shops and offices in Pakistan. Every piece is built to your size, in the wood tone, fabric and finish you choose, and you get a clear price before we start.

Not sure what size or style to choose? Read our furniture buying guide, or send your measurements on WhatsApp and we will suggest a design.

What we build, room by room

Living room

A sofa set is the biggest piece in the room, so we start with it. Our 5 seater sofa set is one three seater and two singles, and you choose the fabric and the colour. Beside it we build a TV unit cut to your wall and a centre table sized to the gap between the sofas.

Bedroom

A double bed with storage is made around your mattress, so the mattress size is the first thing we ask. A dressing table with mirror and side tables can be made in the same finish, so the room looks planned and not collected.

Dining room and office

The 6 seater dining table starts at 180 × 90 cm and can be made longer, shorter or wider. An office desk suits a home study or a small shop counter, with drawers or a keyboard tray if you want them.

Storage

A 3 door wardrobe and a 5 shelf bookshelf are built for the wall they will stand against. Tell us your ceiling height and what you store, and we plan the hanging space and shelves inside.

Measure before you message us

You do not need a drawing. These three things are enough for a first price:

  1. The width, depth and height of the space where the piece will stand, in centimetres.
  2. The width of the doors and stairs on the way in. A big sofa or wardrobe has to get through them.
  3. Your mattress size, if the order is a bed. Brands differ by a few centimetres.

A phone photo of the room helps too. Send it all on WhatsApp at 0311 7420474 and we reply with questions or a price.

Furniture for homes, shops and offices

Most pieces on this site are for homes: a new house, a flat you have just moved into, a bedroom that needs a proper wardrobe. We also build for shops and offices, such as a counter desk or a wall of shelves. If you are furnishing several rooms, send the list and the sizes in one message and we price it together.

Wood, board and finish in plain words

Ask what your piece is made of. This is what the common choices mean:

  • Solid wood such as sheesham or kikar is heavy and strong, and it can be sanded and polished again later. It costs more.
  • Plywood is made of glued layers. It stays flat, so it suits wardrobes and shelves. Quality depends on the glue and the core, so ask about the grade.
  • MDF is pressed wood fibre. It takes paint smoothly, but it swells if it gets wet, so keep it away from damp walls.
  • Veneer is a thin slice of real wood glued onto board. Laminate is a printed plastic layer that resists scratches and stains.
