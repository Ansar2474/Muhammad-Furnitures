Made-to-order furniture in Pakistan

Muhammad Furnitures makes sofa sets, beds, wardrobes, dining tables, office desks, TV units and centre tables for homes, shops and offices in Pakistan. Every piece is built to your size, in the wood tone, fabric and finish you choose, and you get a clear price before we start.

Popular pieces: 5 seater sofa set, double bed with storage, 3 door wardrobe, 6 seater dining table, office desk, TV unit, centre table and dressing table with mirror.

Not sure what size or style to choose? Read our furniture buying guide, or send your measurements on WhatsApp and we will suggest a design.

Questions? We have answers

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

Can you make furniture to my size?

Yes. We build beds, wardrobes, sofas, tables and more to your measurements, so the piece fits your room properly.

How do I get a price?

Send us a photo, your measurements or an idea on WhatsApp (0311 7420474). We reply with a clear price and timeline.

Can I choose the finish and colour?

Yes. Pick a wood tone or colour, or send us a photo of the look you want and we will match it where we can.

What is the price of a sofa set, bed or wardrobe?

Prices start at Rs 22,000 and depend on the size, wood or board, fabric and finish. For example, the Sofa Set (5 Seater) starts at Rs 120,000 and the Wardrobe (3 Door) starts at Rs 85,000. Send us your size and we give you the exact price before we start.

Can you make a full bed set?

Yes. We build beds with storage and dressing tables, and we can plan them to match each other. Tell us which pieces you need and your mattress size.

Do you deliver?

We can deliver and help with setup in Pakistan. Tell us your area when you ask for a quote and we will confirm the details.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes. You can see our latest pieces and customer reviews on our Facebook page and our Google page.
