Bedroom furniture: what to know

A bed is built around the mattress, so the mattress size is the first thing we ask. Brands differ by a few centimetres, and a frame that is slightly too small is hard to live with. Every bedroom piece below can be made to your measurements and finished to match.

Planning a bedroom

Keep about 60 cm free on the sides of the bed you get in and out from. Put the dressing table near the window or a socket, so you have good light and somewhere to plug in a hair dryer.

If the room also needs a wardrobe, see our storage furniture. Making the bed, dressing table and wardrobe in one finish keeps a small room calm.

What you can change

  • Bed size, to fit your mattress.
  • Headboard style, wood tone and finish.
  • How the storage under the bed opens: drawers or a lifting base.
  • Dressing table mirror shape, drawers and a matching stool.

Questions about bedroom furniture

Can the bed be made for a mattress that is not a standard size?

Yes. Send the length and width of your mattress and we build the frame to it.

Is there storage under the bed?

Our double bed with storage has it. Tell us whether you prefer drawers or a lifting base and we plan it with you.

Can I get a dressing table to match the bed?

Yes. We can make the dressing table and side tables in the same wood tone and finish as the bed.
