How to measure your space

A few simple measurements help us give you a quick, accurate quote.

  • Width, depth and height. Measure the space in centimetres where the furniture will stand.
  • Doors and stairs. Note the width of doorways and stairs the piece must pass through.
  • Sockets and windows. Mention anything on the wall that the furniture must work around.
  • Send photos. A photo of the space and any design you like helps us understand your idea.

Can you make furniture to my size?

Yes. We build beds, wardrobes, sofas, tables and more to your measurements, so the piece fits your room properly.

How do I get a price?

Send us a photo, your measurements or an idea on WhatsApp (0311 7420474). We reply with a clear price and timeline.

Can I choose the finish and colour?

Yes. Pick a wood tone or colour, or send us a photo of the look you want and we will match it where we can.

What is the price of a sofa set, bed or wardrobe?

Prices start at Rs 22,000 and depend on the size, wood or board, fabric and finish. For example, the Sofa Set (5 Seater) starts at Rs 120,000 and the Wardrobe (3 Door) starts at Rs 85,000. Send us your size and we give you the exact price before we start.

Can you make a full bed set?

Yes. We build beds with storage and dressing tables, and we can plan them to match each other. Tell us which pieces you need and your mattress size.

Do you deliver?
