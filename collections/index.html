Planning each room

Living room

Start with the sofa, because every other piece depends on it. Measure the wall it will sit against, and keep a walking path of about 80 cm between furniture. Leave around 40 cm between the sofa and the centre table, close enough to reach a cup and far enough to walk through. For the TV unit, tell us the screen size and where the sockets are.

Bedroom

Mattress first, then the room. Keep about 60 cm free on the sides you get in and out of bed. Put the dressing table near the window or a socket, so you have light and a place to plug in a hair dryer.

Dining room

Size the table for the people who eat there every day, not for the biggest dinner you will host once a year. Chairs need room behind them to slide back, so check the distance from the table edge to the wall.

Office and study

A desk about 60 to 70 cm deep suits most screens and keyboards. Leave legroom under the top, and decide where the cables will run before the desk is made. Light from the side is easier on the eyes than light from behind the screen.

Storage

Floors and ceilings are rarely level, so measure the wall height at both ends. A hinged wardrobe door needs space in front of it to open. If the room is tight, tell us and we talk through other layouts. For a bookshelf, say what it will hold, because books and files need different shelf depths.
